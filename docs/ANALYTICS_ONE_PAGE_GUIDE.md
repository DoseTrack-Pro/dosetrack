# Analytics One-Page Guide

This page explains the app's Analytics screen in plain language so you can make faster decisions without guessing.

Think of Analytics as your **"am I on track?" dashboard** for consistency, dose habits, and inventory planning.

---

## How to use this page (30-second version)

1. Pick a range: **7d / 14d / 30d / 90d**.
2. Check **Action needed** first (what needs attention now).
3. Review KPI cards (adherence, logged, missed, streak).
4. Use **Consistency calendar** to see pattern quality at a glance.
5. Scan **Compound scorecards** to spot your weakest link.
6. Check **Inventory outlook** so you do not run dry unexpectedly.
7. Use **Site rotation insight** to keep rotation balanced.

---

## Section-by-section

## 1) Action needed
- This is your priority inbox.
- Shows issues like:
  - missed scheduled doses this week,
  - compounds likely to need refill planning soon,
  - compounds with no logs in 7+ days.
- If you only check one section, check this one.

## 2) KPI summary
- **Adherence**: percent of scheduled doses you actually logged.
- **Logged doses**: how many total dose logs in selected range.
- **Missed doses**: scheduled doses with no matching log.
- **Current streak**: consecutive days with at least one dose log.

Tip: use 7d for weekly check-ins, 14d for behavior tuning, 30d for trend confidence, 90d for bigger-pattern reality checks.

## 3) Consistency calendar
- Each day gets a color status:
  - **Green** = on track
  - **Amber** = partial
  - **Red** = missed
  - **Gray** = not scheduled
- Filter by **All compounds** or a specific compound.
- This is the fastest way to see whether your routine is clean or chaotic.

## 4) Compound scorecards
- Per compound view of:
  - adherence percent,
  - missed count,
  - last log recency,
  - trend vs previous same-length period.
- Sort options:
  - **Most at risk** (best default),
  - **Best**,
  - **A-Z**.

Practical use: if one compound is dragging down your consistency, this section finds it fast.

## 5) Inventory outlook
- Shows which compounds are likely to deplete sooner.
- Displays:
  - remaining doses,
  - days left (or projected timing),
  - urgency color.

Practical use: prevent supply gaps and avoid unplanned protocol interruptions.

## 6) Site rotation insight
- Highlights:
  - most used site,
  - least used site,
  - suggested next site.
- Works best when injection sites are consistently logged.

Practical use: reduce site overuse and keep rotation balanced.

---

## What "good" looks like

- Adherence: **80%+** and improving.
- Missed doses: trending down over time.
- Calendar: mostly green, fewer random red clusters.
- Scorecards: no single compound far behind the rest.
- Inventory: no near-term depletion surprises.

---

## Pro tips (biohacker mode)

- Use **7d** for a quick weekly check.
- Use **14d** after changing protocol habits (quick feedback loop).
- Use **30d** for realistic behavior signal (less noise).
- Use **90d** to catch drift that short windows hide.
- If numbers look wrong, check logging consistency first (especially missed sites or delayed logs).

---

## Important note

Analytics quality depends on logging quality.  
Consistent logs = useful insights.  
Inconsistent logs = misleading confidence.
